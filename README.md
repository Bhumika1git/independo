# independo
A solution for how students can use their skills productively. Based on their level (Beginner/Intermediate),a curated roadmap would be provided which will provide a proper learning/enhancement plan for their particular skill. Further in that roadmap a section is dedicated for how to monetize their particular skill.This in turn decreases the dependency of students for financial factors and help them use their skills productively.
TechStack Used: 1.Streamlit(Python) 2.Embedded HTML/CSS
